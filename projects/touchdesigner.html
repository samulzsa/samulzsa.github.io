Touchdesigner

And Creative Independence

Freelance Teaching2026

TouchDesigner presents a steep learning curve for beginners. Resources are scattered, and without conceptual grounding, students often lose themselves in production without understanding the reasoning behind their decisions.

This course addresses that challenge through a consultation-based approach. Rather than demonstrating how to create systems for specific outcomes, I teach fundamental concepts underlying computer graphics and procedural art. Technical understanding matters, but more important is developing a framework for how to approach an idea before opening the software.

The methodology draws from my own practice: moving from initial concept through research to technical implementation, and finally applying that system to realize artistic vision. This distinction is essential. In procedural design, system building and artistic exploration are separate phases, the idea comes first, then the infrastructure to support it, then the actual creative work within that infrastructure.

It is tempting to stop after completing the system. The technical challenge feels like the accomplishment, The system is scaffolding, not expression. The depth that enables audiences to connect with work emerges only when you use what you built to explore what you wanted to say.

This course shares that lesson. Beyond technical foundations, students develop research skills and the capacity to find their own resources, building toward creative autonomy rather than software dependency.
