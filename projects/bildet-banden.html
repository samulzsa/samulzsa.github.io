Student Teacher2024

This course was structured around cooperative learning, reshaping how we create and grow together through shared process rather than individual output.

I offered my skillset openly; students decided the direction. They chose real-time visuals. To create momentum and real stakes, I proposed a concrete goal: a two-hour live audiovisual performance at a festival. This transformed the course from exercise into genuine collaboration with an audience waiting at the end.

The structure was built on the belief that the right support network transforms hard work into collective growth. Here, failure wasn’t a mark against individual performance, it was an opportunity to learn and adapt as a group.

The focus remained on process: how we tackled challenges together, how individual voices contributed to something larger, how collaboration itself became the primary skill developed.
