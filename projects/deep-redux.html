Deep.Redux

Genius Loci Weimar2023

A ten-minute projection mapping exploring the development of artificial intelligence. The audiovisual work combines data visualization, simulation, and 2D/3D animation, incorporating both contemporary and historical generative AI models. The visual journey spans from early analytical machine learning to deep fakes, super-resolution, and generative models like ChatGPT and Stable Diffusion.

Each collective member contributed visual segments from their individual practice. Unlike other collaborative works developed from shared concepts, this piece brought together distinct artistic expressions under a common narrative. The result was a mixture of styles unified by theme rather than process.

For me, the work brought procedural sculpture practice to a large-scale public context. The experience of seeing work developed on a personal screen projected onto a building facade shifts perception. Scale reveals aspects invisible at desktop size. Familiar forms become unfamiliar. The screening gathered a large audience watching collectively in the dark. Individual reactions were impossible to read, but the shared viewing itself mattered. Prerendered content, yet experienced together in a specific place and time.
