Jonzs

DJ/VJ duosince 2025

Earlier work led me to discover what motion adds to form. But those explorations remained prerendered, frame by frame. Real-time opened different possibilities.

Through years of questioning where value enters art, I arrived at an answer that feels true: shared experience. Unrepeatable moments where artist and audience inhabit the same time, the same space, the same unfolding.

Traditional VJing has largely disappeared from regular club contexts, relocated to festival stages where it functions as background enhancement rather than primary artistic experience. At the same time, audiovisual performance has established itself in art institutions but remains inaccessible to broader audiences despite increasing artistic sophistication. This separation creates a cultural gap. Art becomes increasingly institutionalized while club culture loses access to experimental aesthetic experiences that could transform ordinary social spaces into sites of collective meaning-making.

Jonzs tries to counter this. We perform as a DJ/VJ duo where both artists remain visible on stage, creating a unified aesthetic experience through genuine live collaboration. Our performances combine the accessibility of regular live sets with the artistic depth of audiovisual performance.

Both music and visuals are played live, reacting to each other in real time. Rather than relying on algorithmic audio-reactivity or automated systems, the dynamic emerges from artistic intuition. Two performers listening, responding, shaping a shared moment.
