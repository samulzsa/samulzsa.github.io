Visual Exploration Fundamentals

Student Teacher2023

My teaching centers on a core conviction: technical tools are means for artistic expression, not ends in themselves. This course applied that philosophy to 3D design through Blender.

Rather than working through fundamentals step by step, students jumped directly into creative exploration, learning techniques as they needed them for their own projects. The structure was intentionally unconventional: motivation came from personal interests and questions, not predetermined assignments.

The focus was process over outcome. When students pursued their own visual inquiries, they discovered not just what the software can do, but what they wanted to say with it. The goal wasn’t proficiency, it was finding a voice through sustained experimentation.
