Procedural Sculptures

2022 – 2024

These works exist because of curiosity. Digital space offers what physical sculpture cannot: freedom from material constraint, from gravity, from cost. Forms can float, intersect, repeat at scales that would be impossible to fabricate. This freedom is not escape from sculptural thinking but an extension of it.

The process is procedural. Geometric shapes enter systems that composite them in space, generating iterations faster than deliberate construction would allow. From these outputs, selection happens intuitively. Some forms speak, most don’t. The system proposes, the eye decides.

Movement entered here. Before this work, my practice was static. These videos and loops became a space for discovering what motion adds to form: rhythm, weight, transformation over time. Rendered frame by frame, not yet real-time. That would come later and change everything again.

To some these read as simple 3D visuals. To me they carry the same charge as sculptural exploration, extended through color and time. They don’t critique, don’t position themselves against anything. They exist because making them felt necessary.
